Road Trip Budget Calculator

Fuel, campsites, motels, national parks, and roadside snacks. Know exactly what your road trip will cost before you turn the key.

How It Works

📊

The Math

Fuel = distance ÷ MPG × price per gallon. Accommodation = camping nights × camp rate + motel nights × motel rate. Food = daily budget × travellers × days. National parks = $80 annual pass if 3+ parks, else $35 each. A 10% buffer is added for emergencies.

🎯

How to Use

Enter total distance and unit (miles or km). Input your vehicle's MPG and fuel price. Add trip length, travellers, and accommodation mix. Click Calculate. The pie chart shows fuel, accommodation, food, parks, and buffer. Save routes to compare different trips.

💡

Pro Tips

Save at least 2 routes to use the Compare feature. Camping more nights always lowers cost. The buffer percentage is adjustable — increase it for older vehicles or remote routes. Export your full route report with cost breakdown before you leave.
