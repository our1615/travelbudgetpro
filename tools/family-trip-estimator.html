Family Trip Estimator

Plan a family vacation with realistic costs for kids, accommodation, food, and activities. Compare all-inclusive vs DIY.

How It Works

📊

The Math

We factor in adult costs plus child multipliers by age. Kids under 2 are free. Ages 2-4 add a small daily fee. Ages 5-12 add more. Teenagers add the most. Destination type and trip style (all-inclusive vs DIY) change the base rates significantly.

🎯

How to Use

Enter adults, children, and ages. Pick destination type and trip style. Click Calculate. See total cost, per-person cost, per-day cost, and kids' extra costs. The comparison table shows all three styles side by side so you can pick the cheapest.

💡

Pro Tips

Save up to 5 family trips to compare scenarios. The tool highlights the cheapest style automatically. Theme parks cost more for kids — the calculator adds a park multiplier. Export your budget to share with your partner or travel agent.
