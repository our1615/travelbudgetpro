Digital Nomad Budget Planner

Compare monthly living costs across nomad hubs. See where your remote-work income goes furthest.

How It Works

📊

The Math

We use real cost-of-living data for each nomad hub. Monthly costs include rent, food, transport, co-working, insurance, and misc — adjusted by your accommodation style and co-working days.

🎯

How to Use

Enter your monthly income and co-working days. Pick your accommodation style. Select 2-8 cities to compare. Click Compare Cities. The bar chart shows monthly costs. The table shows rent, food, co-work, and savings per city.

💡

Pro Tips

Save comparisons to track how your options change. The best-value city is highlighted automatically. If your income is higher than costs, the savings column shows how much you could bank each month. Export the full comparison table.
