Multi-City Trip Cost Calculator

Add cities in order. We estimate accommodation, food, local transport, and the cost of getting from city to city.

How It Works

📊

The Math

Each city gets a daily cost index by travel style (budget/mid/luxury). We multiply nights by style rate for accommodation and daily spending. Inter-city transport is estimated based on mode — train, flight, bus, or mixed.

🎯

How to Use

Add cities in trip order. Enter nights per city and travel style. Choose transport mode between cities. Click Calculate. The pie chart breaks down costs by city and transport. The table shows city-by-city detail.

💡

Pro Tips

Save up to 5 itineraries locally. Compare different routes to find the cheapest option. Export your itinerary as a text file with full cost breakdowns. Add or remove cities anytime — just recalculate.
