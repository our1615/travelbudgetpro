Trip Cost Estimator

Log your daily spending by category. Watch your running total and see if you are on track — or heading for trouble.

How It Works

📊

The Math

Set your total budget and trip length. Log daily spending by category. We calculate your running total, remaining budget, and project your final cost based on average daily spend so far.

🎯

How to Use

Enter your total budget and days. Each day, log what you spent on food, accommodation, transport, activities, and misc. The bar chart shows daily spending vs your target. The status tells you if you are on track.

💡

Pro Tips

Data saves automatically in your browser. You can delete individual days or clear everything. Export a full report with daily breakdowns before your trip ends. The projected cost updates every time you log a day.
