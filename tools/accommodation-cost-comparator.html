Accommodation Cost Comparator

Hotels, hostels, and short-term rentals — with cleaning fees, taxes, and resort fees included. See the real total before you book.

How It Works

📊

The Math

Hotel total = nightly rate × nights + resort fees × nights + tax + parking. Hostel total = private room × nights + extras. Rental total = nightly × nights + cleaning fee + service fee + tax. We show the real total, not the advertised rate.

🎯

How to Use

Enter nights and guests. Fill in the hotel rate with resort fees, tax, and parking. Add hostel private room rate and extras. Enter rental nightly rate with cleaning fee, service fee percentage, and tax. Click Compare. The bar chart shows true totals. The cheapest option is highlighted.

💡

Pro Tips

Always include resort fees and cleaning fees — they can add 30% to the advertised price. For short stays (1-2 nights), hotels often beat rentals because cleaning fees are spread over fewer nights. Save comparisons to revisit before booking. Export the full fee breakdown.
