Currency Budget Converter

See how exchange rates affect your travel budget. Convert between currencies and track rate changes over time.

How It Works

📊

The Math

We use approximate mid-market exchange rates against USD as the base. Enter your home currency and destination currency. If you leave the rate blank, we auto-fill. If you have a real rate from your bank, enter it manually.

🎯

How to Use

Enter your trip budget amount. Select from and to currencies. Optionally enter a custom exchange rate. Click Convert. See your converted amount, daily budget, and a 30-day simulated rate trend. Set a rate alert to know when to buy currency.

💡

Pro Tips

Use the Rate Impact Simulator to see how a 5% or 10% rate swing affects your budget. Save conversions to compare over time. If the rate drops below your alert threshold, you will know it is a good time to exchange. Always check your bank's actual rate before buying.
