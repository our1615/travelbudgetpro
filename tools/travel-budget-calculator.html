Travel Budget Calculator

Enter your destination, trip length, and travel style. Get a realistic daily budget and total cost estimate in seconds.

How It Works

📊

The Math

We aggregate real booking data from thousands of trips. Each region has a daily cost index for budget, mid-range, and luxury travel. Multiply by days and travellers for your total.

🎯

How to Use

Pick your destination region, trip length, and travel style. Click Calculate. The pie chart shows where your money goes — accommodation, food, transport, activities, and misc.

💡

Pro Tips

Save multiple budgets to compare destinations. Use the Compare button to see side-by-side costs. Export your report as a text file to share with your travel group.
