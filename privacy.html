Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Travel Budget Pro respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information.

We do not collect personal data through our budgeting tools. All calculations run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. We never see your budget numbers, destinations, or travel plans.

We may use basic analytics to understand how visitors navigate the site. This data is anonymised and does not identify you personally.

Our articles may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

4. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email privacy@travelbudgetpro.org.
